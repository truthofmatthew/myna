How it works

Phone → Mac → Meet

Three small pieces that only talk to each other.

Phone

Myna for Android

Records the camera and mic and sends them over your Wi-Fi, encrypted, in 720p at 30 fps.

→
Mac

Myna for macOS

Receives the stream and serves it only to this computer, on 127.0.0.1.

→
Browser

This extension

Adds the phone to Meet's camera and mic lists and decodes the video right in the tab.

You need the free Myna apps on the Mac and the phone. The extension alone does nothing.
